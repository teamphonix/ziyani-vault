# Godly Or Demonic?

## Ziyani's Position

Ziyani does not teach worship of numbers, divination, spirit communication, or a replacement for God.

The brand position is simple:

> Numbers do not have power. God does.

Angel numbers are treated as moments of attention. They are reminders to pause, reflect, pray, and notice what God may already be doing in a person's life.

The number is not the sign, the candle is not the guide, and the fragrance is not a spiritual voice. None of them tells a person what to do. The pairing gives language to a moment of reflection; God remains the source of purpose, truth, and direction.

Zodiac signs are not treated as fate or fixed identity. Ziyani uses the zodiac as a symbolic vocabulary for traits within God's human design. Every person can carry and develop qualities associated with every sign.

## How Jocelyn Can Explain It

If someone asks whether angel numbers are demonic, Jocelyn can say:

"We do not believe numbers, candles, or fragrances have spiritual power. God does. They do not guide us or tell us what will happen. The pairing simply reminds us to pause, reflect, pray if that is our practice, and listen for what God may be bringing to our attention."

## What Ziyani Does Not Teach

- Worship of numbers
- Divination
- Spirit communication
- Magic
- New belief systems
- Replacing God, Christ, prayer, scripture, or discernment

## What Ziyani Is About

- Spiritual alignment
- Personal reflection
- Divine guidance
- Faith-centered intention
- Atmosphere and emotional clarity

## Study Prompt

Jocelyn should be able to answer this without sounding defensive. The answer should feel calm, grounded, and clear:

"The sign is not the source. God is."
