# Ziyani Brand Operating System

*Created: 2026-05-08*

## Name History

The live brand spelling is **Ziyani**.

**Lumea** was the original working name. Any file still using Lumea should be treated as legacy source material and renamed or rewritten before public use.

## Core Concept

Ziyani is an intention-based candle and ritual fragrance brand built around a modular alignment system:

- **Zodiac sign = base fragrance.** The person's energetic nature, temperament, and self-expression.
- **Angel number = accent fragrance.** The timing, lesson, or energy the person wants to embody.
- **Combined candle = lived alignment.** A scent and mood created from identity meeting current intention.

The brand phrase is:

> Light your alignment.

The product system creates **108 candle combinations**:

- 12 zodiac base fragrances
- 9 angel-number accent fragrances
- 108 total zodiac + angel number pairings

## Brand Promise

Ziyani gives customers a candle that is not just decorative. Each candle is a reflection tool: a scent they choose because it mirrors who they are and what they are moving through.

When someone lights a Ziyani candle, the message is:

- I am choosing alignment.
- I am naming the energy I want to embody.
- I am creating an atmosphere that supports that energy.

## Tone

The brand should feel:

- elegant
- spiritual without being heavy
- personal without being chaotic
- sensory
- refined
- emotionally intelligent
- intimate
- premium but accessible

Avoid language that feels too clinical, too occult, too vague, or too over-explained. The label language should be short, memorable, and emotionally clear.

## Design Rules

The Libra line has established the production model:

- Each zodiac gets 9 candles, one for each angel number.
- The **angel-number color scheme stays consistent across every zodiac**.
- The zodiac imagery changes by sign.
- Sagittarius 111 should use the same color family as Libra 111, but with Sagittarius-specific imagery.
- Sagittarius 222 should use the same color family as Libra 222, and so on.

This makes the system easy to recognize:

- number = color world
- zodiac = symbol/image world
- pairing title + three words = emotional purpose

## Label Content System

Each label needs:

- Brand: Ziyani
- Zodiac sign
- Angel number
- Pairing title
- Base fragrance
- Accent fragrance
- Three attributes
- Optional short affirmation or product copy for web/catalog use

Example from Libra:

```text
ZIYANI
LIBRA | 555
FLUID BALANCE

Rose & Bergamot
+ Fresh Air Salted Woods
Calm | Open | Balanced
```
