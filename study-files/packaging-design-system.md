# Ziyani Packaging Design System — Working Standard

## Purpose

The packaging system is built so all 108 candles remain visually consistent while each zodiac and angel-number pairing keeps its own identity. AI artwork is treated as an editable source asset, not as the finished package.

## Customer-facing product hierarchy

Every candle should present:

1. Ziyani
2. Zodiac
3. Angel number
4. Blend name
5. Pairing definition
6. Three defining words
7. Expanded meaning / reflection

Fragrance ingredients and individual scent notes are **not** part of the primary customer-facing label system. Formula and ingredient details remain internal production information.

## Box faces

### Front — Hero identity

- Ziyani logo
- Approved zodiac avatar or zodiac artwork
- Zodiac name
- Angel number
- Blend name
- Optional zodiac glyph / crest
- “Light Your Alignment” when space allows

The front should feel restrained, premium, and immediately recognizable. Avoid placing long definitions or ingredient lists here.

### Side A — Three words

Feature the three approved words that define the zodiac + angel-number pairing. They should be prominent enough to function as the fast emotional read of the candle.

### Side B — Expanded meaning

Expand the three words into concise emotional language. Explain what the candle symbolizes and how the zodiac identity changes when paired with that angel number.

### Back — Pairing reflection

Use a short reflection centered on the approved pairing definition. The reflection should feel intentional, calm, sophisticated, and customer-facing rather than technical.

Close with:

> Light Your Alignment

### Top — Recognition mark

Use a minimal recognition system:

- Zodiac glyph or approved crest
- Angel number

### Bottom — Safety / production

Reserve this face for required or practical information such as:

- Net weight
- Candle warning / burn guidance
- Business information
- Batch or production identifiers when needed
- Other legally required information

## AI artwork workflow

AI should generate **artwork assets**, not flattened final packaging.

Keep these elements separate and editable:

- Character artwork
- Background / celestial environment
- Zodiac symbol / crest
- Decorative motifs
- Logo
- Angel number
- Blend name
- Pairing definition
- Three words
- Typography
- Legal / safety text

This prevents spelling, font, logo, spacing, and consistency problems across the 108-package system.

## Pairing design slots

Each zodiac receives nine design slots:

- 111
- 222
- 333
- 444
- 555
- 666
- 777
- 888
- 999

Every slot must inherit its approved blend name, definition, and three defining words from the Vault before design work begins.

## Current development rule

The Vault is the source of truth for the pairing identity. Packaging, product pages, future content, and marketing copy should be generated from the same approved pairing record rather than maintained independently.
