# Jocelyn Starter Package

*Created: 2026-05-08*

## Objective

Jocelyn's first job is not to make 108 sellable candles. Her first job is to become dangerous with the fundamentals:

- safe pouring
- accurate measuring
- consistent wick placement
- wax and fragrance behavior
- burn testing
- hot throw and cold throw
- label fit
- production notes
- repeatable batches

The first $500-$1,000 should buy learning, testing, and repeatability, not inventory bloat.

## 30-Day Training Path

### Week 1: Foundation

- Watch beginner soy/blend candle-making lessons.
- Learn wax melt temperature, fragrance oil temperature, pour temperature, cure time, and wick sizing.
- Set up a clean workspace with hot zone, clean zone, and cure/QC zone.
- Choose one starter vessel size for testing.
- Choose one wax type or wax blend to start.

Deliverable: a written mini-SOP Jocelyn can follow without guessing.

### Week 2: First Controlled Test Batch

- Make unscented candles first to learn wick placement and pouring.
- Make 3-6 single-fragrance candles with the Libra base fragrance.
- Track grams of wax, grams of fragrance, pour temperature, room temperature, wick size, vessel, cure date, and burn notes.

Deliverable: first batch log with photos and honest notes.

### Week 3: Base + Accent Testing

- Test Libra base with 2-3 angel accents first, not all 9.
- Start with 111, 444, and 888 because they give different scent behaviors: bright, grounded, warm.
- Compare cold throw, hot throw, flame size, melt pool, tunneling, soot, and customer appeal.

Deliverable: first proof that the modular system works in wax, not just on paper.

### Week 4: Presentation Practice

- Create 3 finished sample candles.
- Practice explaining "Light your alignment" in under 30 seconds.
- Practice telling the Libra story without overloading the customer.
- Photograph candles in clean natural light.
- Decide what is ready, what needs retesting, and what should wait.

Deliverable: 3-camera-ready sample candles and Jocelyn's first sales explanation.

## What Not To Do Yet

- Do not buy full-size quantities for all 108 candles.
- Do not launch every zodiac at once.
- Do not change wax, wick, vessel, and fragrance load all in the same test.
- Do not assume the label design is final until it is printed and wrapped on the actual vessel.
- Do not sell untested candles.

## Practice Metrics

Each test candle should be judged on:

- appearance after pour
- scent strength before burn
- scent strength while burning
- melt pool after 2 hours
- melt pool after 4 hours
- flame height
- soot/smoke
- tunneling
- jar temperature
- surface after cooling
- whether the scent story matches the label words
