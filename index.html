Brand Foundation

Ziyani is a faith-aware ritual fragrance brand built for reflection, alignment, and atmosphere.

The zodiac is the base energy. The angel number is the accent energy. The candle is the lived experience: identity meeting intention through scent.

What The Brand Is

  • A modular candle system: 12 zodiac bases and 9 angel-number accents.
  • A private reflection tool customers can use to name the energy they want to embody.
  • A sensory brand rooted in beauty, emotional clarity, intention, and God-centered awareness.
  • A future lifestyle space, not just a candle shelf.

What The Brand Is Not

  • Not worship of numbers.
  • Not spirit communication.
  • Not divination.
  • Not a replacement for God, Christ, prayer, discernment, or scripture.

Godly Or Demonic?

Grounding

Everything begins and ends with God. Ziyani does not teach that numbers have power. God has power.

Angel Numbers

Numbers are treated as moments of attention: pause, reflect, pray, and notice what God may already be doing.

Biblical Pattern

Scripture uses repetition, numbers, signs, seasons, and symbols to call people into awareness. The sign is not the source. God is.

Simple Answer

We do not believe numbers have power. God does. The numbers remind us to pause, reflect, and seek Him.
